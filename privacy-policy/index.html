Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

This app ("TILs Scoring App" / "BC TILs Scoring") does not collect, store, or transmit any personal data or user information.

The app functions entirely offline. Its reference example images for scoring Tumour-Infiltrating Lymphocytes (TILs) in breast cancer histopathology slides are bundled with the app itself, so no internet connection or external server is needed to view them.

The app can also use your device's camera or photo library so you can view your own photo locally within the app, side by side with the reference images. That photo is never uploaded, transmitted, or shared — it stays on your device.

The app does not require account creation, does not use analytics or tracking tools, and does not collect any user data.
